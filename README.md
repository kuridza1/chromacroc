ChromaCroc
Google Developer Group Hackaton Build with AI solution
Problem Solving for AI for All

Did you know that crocodiles have a red-green vision deficiency? Our application ChromaCroc explores better ways of helping the color blind community use our AI assistent that takes into considiration context clues, shadows, opacity and color blind diagnosis while delivering short and clear answer to the important question: What color is this?
Application doesn't help only crocodiles though. People who struggle with deciding whether meat is coocked enough since they don't differentiate most nuances of red shades can sit back and relax while our assistent checks for them and gives a coherent answer to any of their questions regarding color.

Technical side
App is made with Android Studio and uses AI Studio from Google AI Development Kit as the agents responsible for smart answers
